## 📌 Overview

Sentinel Nova is an AI-powered productivity platform designed to help individuals and teams intelligently plan, prioritize, and execute work before deadlines are missed.

Unlike traditional AI assistants that simply answer questions, Sentinel Nova acts as an **AI Chief of Staff**, combining multi-agent reasoning, predictive intelligence, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and workflow automation to proactively assist users in achieving their goals.

The platform was developed as a modern AI productivity system demonstrating production-ready AI engineering practices using Large Language Models, multi-agent orchestration, and scalable backend architecture.
